v1su4rt · ESF-01

Web experimentI explore digital pigment through an interactive shader that responds to pointer and touch input.

I create a generative image in the browser with GLSL ES 1.0 shaders. The base is gray. Each touch brings a patch of pigment to life, spreading and dissolving back into gray.

Inputs
Pointer and touch
Graphics
GLSL ES 1.0 with a Canvas2D fallback

03 · Journey

About

I’m Colombian and have lived in São Paulo since 2014. I work across audiovisual art, video mapping, photography, scenography, installations and interactive systems. My practice is intertwined with Ouvidor 63 and research into autonomous and nomadic artists. Since 2019, I’ve been part of Sucata Quântica, where I create scenography, exhibition designs and lighting devices with repurposed materials. The question guiding my work is how image, sound and light can activate spaces, memories and forms of collective creation.

Education

2009 · 2010
Graphic Design; Artistic Techniques, Corporación Escuela de Artes y Letras (Bogotá).
2010–2013
Fine and Visual Arts, ASAB, Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas.
2016–2019
Teaching degree in Visual Arts, Estácio de Sá (São Paulo).
2023–2025
Master’s studies in Art History, Unifesp, with a FAPESP scholarship.

The ambient experience is part of my work

The background of this page is part of my work, too. Five visual languages — smoke, analog noise, organic form, flow and mesh — transform as you scroll down. Tap or drag outside the sheet: pigment appears, spreads and dissolves back into gray. Reading never depends on the effect.
